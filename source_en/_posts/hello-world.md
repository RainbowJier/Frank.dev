This is my first blog post. After some time, I finally have a small corner of the internet that is fully mine and under my control. This article is about what this site is, why I made it, and what I plan to do with it.

## Why a personal website

Content on social media fades with platform changes — links break, accounts get banned, algorithms decide who sees your words. Your own website doesn't. It's like a home on the internet:

- Express yourself fully, without word limits or format constraints;
- Notes and thoughts from learning find a place to settle;
- When hunting for a job or collaboration, it's the best business card.

## How this site is built

The whole site is static: HTML, CSS and a bit of JavaScript. No servers, no databases, no build tools. This is a deliberate choice —

> A static site is the simplest, most reliable form: any hosting platform can run it, it rarely breaks, and edits publish instantly.

If you want to build a similar site, you only need three files:

```
Frank.dev/
├── index.html    # homepage
├── css/style.css # styles
└── js/main.js    # a little interaction
```
